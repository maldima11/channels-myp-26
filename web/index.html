Portal Usage Guide

  1. Access Paths: Choose Farmer Portal or Officer Console at start.
  2. Ward Selection: Select location ward from the dropdown to automatically load biophysical defaults.
  3. Cultivar Selection: Select a maize cultivar to match model calibration.
  4. Biophysical Scenarios: Manually adjust Precipitation, Heat, Sand, and Clay sliders to simulate specific local soil/weather stressors.
  5. Meters & Gauges: Monitor Water Deficit and Heat Stress gauges in real-time.
  6. Yield Forecast Chart: View Low (q10), Median (q50), and High (q90) yield predictions.
  7. Agronomic Advisory: Read specific agricultural advice and click Download Report to export a full HTML/PDF formatted summary.
